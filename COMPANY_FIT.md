# Poke-Switch — Company and Team Fit

Potential employer/skills matches only; no partnership, endorsement or product-use claim is implied.

## Project thesis

A community website where players can browse official Pokémon games and suggest or vote for game releases and features they would like to see.

## GitHub

**Relevant technical areas:** Community platforms and open-source development.

**Potential roles:** Full-stack Engineer, Frontend Engineer, Community Platform Engineer, Trust & Safety Engineer or Product Engineer.

**Evidence to build:** Browser-based catalogue and request/voting concept with official Pokémon/Nintendo links and references to independent fan projects. It must remain an unofficial fan project, respect trademarks/copyright, and distinguish official products from community fan creations. Do not host ROMs, copyrighted game assets or unauthorised downloads.
